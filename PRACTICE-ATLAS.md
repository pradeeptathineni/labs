## Other forms of practice

- **Architecture:** Defend a system design: requirements, alternatives, constraints and failure analysis. A close complement to infrastructure and customer-facing architecture work. Solo critique is not independent peer review.
- **Research:** Answer a bounded question with corroboration, provenance, contradictions and limits. Scripts may support the investigation; the evidence-backed answer is the result.
- **Writing:** Improve clarity or an argument deliberately. Compare drafts and explain revisions. A README that supports a code lab does not need a second physical home.
- **Teaching:** Design an explanation, activity and assessment that support understanding. Connect STEM teaching and mentoring to actual learner feedback when available; rehearsal alone does not prove delivery.
- **Design:** Explore human-facing experiences later. Distinguish a solo prototype from user research with real participants.
- **Decisions:** Explore defended choices and forecasting later. Calibration requires resolved outcomes, and thoughtful decisions already belong throughout the repository.
